# Foreign Hit Translator: Company Profile

---

## 🌍 Mission Statement

**Democratize global music by breaking down cultural and linguistic barriers through AI-powered translation, collaboration, and viral prediction.**

We believe every artist, regardless of location or label backing, deserves the opportunity to reach a global audience. We're building the infrastructure that makes this possible.

---

## 🎯 Company Overview

**Founded:** 2024  
**Headquarters:** San Francisco, CA (with global remote team)  
**Industry:** Music Technology / AI / SaaS  
**Stage:** Series A  

**What We Do:**
Foreign Hit Translator is an AI-powered platform that enables independent musicians to:
- Translate songs across languages while preserving melodic fit and emotional impact
- Predict viral potential 6 months before mainstream breakthrough
- Create cross-cultural mashups and genre fusions
- Collaborate with artists worldwide
- Bypass major label gatekeepers while keeping 96% of revenue

## 💡 Company Values

### 🎵 **Artists First**
Everything we build starts with the question: "Does this empower independent artists?" If the answer isn't a resounding yes, we don't build it.

### 🌐 **Cultural Respect**
We celebrate cultural diversity and approach every translation, adaptation, and fusion with deep respect for the source material and culture.

### 🤖 **AI as Amplifier, Not Replacement**
AI is a tool to amplify human creativity, never to replace it. Artists maintain full creative control. Always.

### 🔓 **Radical Transparency**
From algorithm decisions to revenue splits to prediction methods - we operate in the open. Artists deserve to know how the platform works.

### 🚀 **Speed of Culture**
Music culture moves fast. We move faster. Ship early, learn quickly, improve constantly.

### 🌈 **Inclusive by Design**
Our platform works for artists from Lagos to Tokyo, from bedroom producers to rising stars. If it doesn't work globally, it doesn't work.
